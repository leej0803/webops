H.COM LARGE PROJECTS | PROCESS

Project Information

PREP JOB INFORMATION

  • HMA: Podio Request due from client
  • BM: Assess Podio submission
  • BM: Write assignment summary
  • BM/DP/PM: Add to roadmap
    • Team assignment
    • Project Prioritization
    • Target live date
    • Rough timeline
    • Product guide
    • Asset availability dates
  • BM: Send JRF to PM to open job

KICK-OFF/BRIEFING

  • DP: Schedule Kick-off meeting/briefing with team
  • BM: Provide brief and known assets
  • BM: List out unknowns (ie: URL) and send brief to TSA/Data Science
  • DP/WebOps: Consult with HAEA and team on feasibility
  • PM: Engage CGI + Retouching (if needed)
  • DP: Create project plan and populate in roadmap
  • CG Producer: Provide timing on assets

Creative Development

CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT

  • C: Copywriting & Design
  • DP: Set up creative reviews with CD - check with PM on availability
  • PM: Set up creative reviews ECD as needed
  • DP: Route creative through PP and Proofing

IWA INTERNAL REVIEWS

  • PM/DP: Set up internal review meeting. Verify all parties have seen the work. (BM, CD/ECD, PP, Proofing).
  • DP/PM: Route creative through core team (if needed)

CLIENT REVIEW

  • BM: Set up client review meeting. Send to HMA Product/HMA Legal/D&G/TSA if needed
  • DP/PM: Route creative through HAEA/Data Science as needed

CREATIVE DEVELOPMENT REVISIONS

  • C: Copywriting & Design revisions
  • DP: Set up creative reviews with CD - check with PM on availability
  • DP: Route creative through PP and Proofing
  • PM/DP: Set up internal review meeting. Verify all parties have approved the work. (BM, CD/ECD, PP, Proofing, Core Team).
  • BM: Ensure HMA Legal/HMA Product/D&G has approved

CLIENT REVIEW 2

  • BM: Set up client review meeting. Re-send to HMA Product/HMA Legal/D&G/TSA if needed
  • DP/PM: Re-route creative through HAEA/Data Science as needed

ALL ASSETS APPROVED

  • Confirm Client sign off/approval on: COPYDECKS AND FINAL COMPS
  • DP/PM: Route creative through core team (if needed)
  • BM/DP: Send final comps & copy decks to TSA for metadata/keywords. Confirm URLs to be used. Confirm approved URLs are in the copy decks.

Pre-Production

CONTENT PRODUCTION

  • DP: Create estimate, send through to EVP, send for client approval
  • DP: Engage PA
  • PA: Asset creation
  • DP: Confirm and review assets/URLs
    • Dimensions
    • Final assets
    • Disclaimers/Legal copy etc.
    • Confirm with TSA/Data Science/HAEA on approved URL/mobile redirects/etc.

Content Publishing & QA

WEBOPS KICK-OFF

  • DP, WebOps & HAEA: Discuss
    • Asset deliveries
    • Copydecks deliveries
    • Schedule
    • Notes and Requirements
  • DP: Posts final assets on server and creates JIRA "EPIC"
  • DP: Creates a ticket for TSA to confirm URLs

CLIENT STAGING REVIEW (SWEB)

  • BM: Sends staging links to HMA for approval
  • HMA Marketing: Provides consolidated feedback if necessary
  • DP: TSA confirms URLs are correct via JIRA ticket
  • DP: Creates client feedback issue in Jira
  • BM/DP: Prioritization of feedback:
    • For live date
    • For running changes

PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT

  • WebOps (& HAEA) Publishers: Deploy to production
  • WebOps (& HAEA) QA: Perform post production QA
  • BM: Notifies HMA deployment is completed with post production QA
  • DP: TSA confirms URLs are correct in production

Production Complete
